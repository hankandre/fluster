# Contrast report

Generated by `pnpm test:visual` from the worst-case artwork captures (P13-30); do not edit by hand. Each capture is taken twice: once as shown, and once with text hidden and text shadows removed, so the pixels behind every visible text run can be read. Scrims and gradients stay in the picture. The measured ratio is the 5th percentile of the per-pixel contrast between the text (with its opacity) and the pixels behind it, so 95% of the background meets it; the lowest ratio is shown for context. Thresholds follow DESIGN.md section 11 rule 1: 3:1 for 24 px and up or 18.66 px at weight 700 and up, otherwise 4.5:1. Text is not tested for occlusion by other elements, and non-text contrast is out of scope.
