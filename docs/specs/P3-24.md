# P3-24: Add skip settings for intros and credits

Task: `P3-24` in `tasks/prd.json` (domain Playback Session, model Sonnet, skill `verify`, `human: false`).
Depends on P3-22 (preferences store) and P3-11 (skip from media segments); both are `done` with `passes: true` on `origin/main` (checked 2026-11-16 at `ed7631d`).
Implementer: **`implementer`** (TypeScript only). Nothing under `src-tauri/` and no PlayerEngine port change.

## Goal

Let the viewer choose, once per computer, what happens at an intro and at the credits: show a Skip button (today's behaviour and the default), skip automatically, or do nothing (product review Q14 A, `docs/plans/proposals/product-review.md` line 60 and lines 606-614). Automatic skip seeks past a segment once per playback and says so in the OSD; Up Next still takes the place of Skip credits when a next episode exists (Q19 A, `DESIGN.md` §7.15).

## Approach

1. **Domain.** In `media-segments.ts`, add the types and three pure functions:
   - `offeredSegments(segments, modes, hasNext)`: map `Intro` to `modes.intro` and `Outro` to `modes.credits`; drop every `Outro` when `hasNext` (Q19 A); put `"button"` segments in `button`, `"auto"` in `auto`, drop `"none"`.
   - `segmentKey(s)`: `` `${s.kind}:${s.startSeconds}:${s.endSeconds}` ``.
   - `segmentToAutoSkip(segments, position, done)`: `activeSegment(segments, position)` restricted to segments whose key is not in `done`. Reusing `activeSegment`'s window means a position in the last `SKIP_HIDE_BEFORE_END_SECONDS` never triggers.
   - Decision (refiner, from the acceptance wording "seeks past the segment once"): the first time the position is inside an auto segment, by playback, by a resume start, or by a seek that lands there, it skips; every later entry in the same item does not. This keeps one simple rule; a viewer who wants the intro seeks back once.
2. **Store.** Add `skipModes` to `PreferenceValues` with `sanitizeSkipModes(value)` validating each kind on its own against `SKIP_MODES` (so `{ intro: "auto", credits: 7 }` reads `{ intro: "auto", credits: "button" }`). Add `skipModesOf`. Export `skipModes` from `browser-preference-store.ts`, thread it through `AppServices`, `App.tsx`, and `Preferences`.
3. **Settings.** `skip-rows.tsx` exports `SkipRows()`, rendering the two rows from `usePreferences().skipModes` with `useSyncExternalStore` (as `home-section.tsx` does) and a `SkipModePicker` modelled on `WindowPicker` (`hideLabel`, `rovingItem`, `openOnArrow={false}`, `describedBy={descriptionId}`). A change to the same value does nothing; a new value calls `set` and shows the toast. `PlaybackSection` renders `<SkipRows />` between the limit rows and Audio passthrough. Leave the section intro as is.
4. **Player wiring.** In `PlaybackScreen` (`src/features/player/index.tsx`) compute the split with `Boolean(next)` and pass both lists. In `PlayerView`, call `useAutoSkip({ itemId: item.id, segments: autoSkipSegments, snapshot, ready: session.firstFrameReady && !sessionError, seek: (s) => execute(() => player.seek(s)), showOsd: osd.show })`. The hook keeps a `Set<string>` in a ref, cleared when `itemId` changes; in an effect on `snapshot?.positionSeconds`, it finds `segmentToAutoSkip`, adds its key, calls `seek(segment.endSeconds)`, then `showOsd(skippedCopy(segment.kind))` (key slot). It does not retry after a failed seek. Because auto segments never reach `segments`, there is no Skip button, no `s`, and no TV OK for them.
5. **OSD copy.** `skippedCopy(kind)` returns `"Skipped intro"` for `Intro` and `"Skipped credits"` for `Outro`.
6. **Docs.** `DESIGN.md` §7.14, §7.15, §7.18 and `docs/security.md` as listed. Mention the new captures in the PR.
7. **Tests and captures** per the Test plan; accept baselines with `pnpm test:visual:accept` after review of the diff images.

## Risks and out of scope

- **Merge overlap with P3-23** (auto-play settings, `todo`): both add a field to `PreferenceValues`, a member to `Preferences`, and rows to `playback-section.tsx`. Whichever lands second rebases; keeping the skip rows in `skip-rows.tsx` limits the conflict to one line.
