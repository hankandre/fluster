## Goal

Changing a setting today means stopping, going Home, opening Settings, and starting again (owner request, `tasks/backlog.md:405-412`). The owner's words: "you would be able to change settings while streaming and not have to leave your viewing experience to do that." This task adds a fourth player sheet, **Settings**, on the same `PlayerSheet` as Info, Audio & Subtitles, and Chapters. It renders the Settings page's own section components, reached through the page's compact drill-in form (DESIGN.md §7.18), with each row's control under its text.

This task ships Playback (auto-play, Countdown, Intros, Credits, Audio passthrough as unavailable), Home, Subtitles, Account, and About & diagnostics. The two streaming limit rows arrive with P3-33 and the Languages section with P3-34, because those are the changes that act on the stream. Nothing in P3-32 sends a playback request.

Auto-play stops being read once per session (`src/features/player/index.tsx:136-137`) and applies at once, with a stated rule for a change made while Up Next is counting. Skip modes already apply at once (`index.tsx:33-37`, `:199-201`). Every confirmation goes to the OSD, since toasts are dropped on the player route (`src/app/App.tsx:83`, `src/features/shared/ui/toast.tsx:56-58`). Sign out from the sheet stops playback the way Back does, reports the position, and only then revokes the sign-in.

The task also clears the sheet-focus `preventScroll` lows from #174 (`player-sheet.tsx:47`, `:58`; `info-sheet.tsx:89`) and the two timing-dependent visual captures from the P3-24 ship notes (`54c`, `55c`; `tasks/backlog.md:419`).
