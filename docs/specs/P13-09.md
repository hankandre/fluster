## Goal

Make the detail screen read like the item, not like a form. Today the page `h1` is "Details" and the title is an `h2` (`src/features/detail/item-detail-view.tsx:27`, `:86`). A small poster sits in a 960 px column (`src/styles.css:121`). Watched and Favorite are unstyled text buttons whose state is colour only (`watch-state.tsx:51`). Versions are native radios at the bottom of the page (`media-info.tsx:6`, `item-detail-view.tsx:110`). Cast is a plain list. Not found is one sentence with a link (`item-detail-view.tsx:56`). See audit.md "Item detail" (P0, four P1s, three P2s) and Top 3 and Top 4.

After this task:

- The item's Logo (movies) or title is the `h1`. It sits over the full-bleed backdrop in the left five columns with a facts line, `MediaBadges`, the `ActionBar`, and a three-line overview with a "More" disclosure (DESIGN.md §7.7).
- The `ActionBar` (§7.8, amended by Q6 A and Q7 A) holds Resume (with a progress ring) or Play, the version `Picker` pill when there are two or more versions, Start over when resuming, and the Watched and Favorite toggles with icon state and `aria-pressed`. There is no `More` menu.
- A `File and streams` section at the bottom holds the chosen version's media info (§7.9).
- Cast is a `PersonRow` of initials cards (Q8 A).
- An episode shows its series as a link above the title, the facts "S1 · E2 · 45 min", and a "More from Season 1" strip of the season's other episodes. Enter on a strip card opens that episode's detail (Q9 A).
- A missing item shows a `Notice`: "This item is no longer on the server" with "Go home" (§10).
- Follow-ups from P13-08 that name this task land here: the progress ring on Resume (detail and the Home hero), and the §9 300 ms detail prefetch on Home.

## Approach

### 1. Page frame and states (`item-detail-view.tsx`)

`ItemDetailView({ itemId })` replaces `BrowseFrame`. It does the following:

- It reads `useSession()` and redirects to `#/next/sign-in` without a gateway, as `BrowseFrame` does (`browse-view.tsx:24`).
- It renders `<main className="detail-page">`. `.detail-page` sets `position: relative; z-index: var(--z-content)` (the stacking context that `.home` gave; see the P13-08 spec-check note 2) and `--ambient-h: var(--detail-header-h)`.
- It keeps the `ItemDetail` → `AmbientBackdrop` → `ItemDetailContent key={itemId}` structure, so items crossfade (`item-detail-backdrop.test.tsx` stays). The art width uses `backdropWidth()` from `card-view.ts` instead of the fixed 1280.

`ItemDetailContent` switches on the query state. The query is unchanged: `detailKey`, `gcTime: 0`.

- Pending: a skeleton shaped like the header, with an `h1` block, a facts line, and two control-height blocks. A visually hidden `<h1>Loading details</h1>` and `aria-busy="true"` go on the header. Skeletons show after 150 ms through `Skeleton`, as §7.17 requires.
- `ItemNotFoundError`: `Notice` (Approach 10).
- Other error: `<h1 className="visually-hidden">Couldn't load this item</h1>` plus `InlineError message="Couldn't load this item." onRetry`.
- Success: `DetailBody`.

Focus: a `useHeadingFocus(itemId, state)` hook focuses the rendered `h1` (`tabIndex={-1}`) once per item, when the state first becomes success, not-found, or error. It never focuses during pending, so screen readers hear the title once (§7.8: "on desktop, focus goes to the `h1` first"). One Tab then reaches the series link (episodes) or the primary action.

The developer link "Back to home" and the route-named `h1` "Details" go away (DESIGN.md §12: "Route-named pages" and "developer links in flows"). The nav reaches Home, and browser Back works as before.

### 2. Art (`detail-art.ts`)

`detailArt(item)` returns `{ ambient?: ImageRef, tint?: string, inlinePoster: boolean }`:

- `ambient`: `item.backdrop`. For an `Episode` without a backdrop, it is `item.poster` (the episode still, §7.7).
- `tint`: the BlurHash of `ambient ?? item.poster` (as today, `item-detail-view.tsx:70`).
- `inlinePoster`: true when `ambient` is undefined and `item.poster` exists. That is a movie without a backdrop: the poster sits beside the text at `--poster-w` with a 2:3 frame.

On compact windows (`max-width: 719px`), `.detail-page` sets `--ambient-h: 0px`, so only the tint remains. The header shows the art inline as a full-width frame: 16:9 for `ambient`, 2:3 at `--poster-w` for a poster-only item. Then come the title, the actions, and the overview (§7.7, compact). This matches the P13-08 hero's compact stacking.

### 3. `DetailHeader` (shared, presentational)

Props:

- `title: string`
- `logo?: { src: string }`
- `seriesLink?: { name: string; href?: string }`
- `facts?: string`
- `badges?: ReactNode`
- `actions: ReactNode`
- `notice?: ReactNode`
- `overview?: ReactNode`
- `inlineArt?: { src: string; blurHash?: string; ratio: "16 / 9" | "2 / 3" }`
- `headingRef`

Markup and layout:

- `<header className="detail-header">`, with `min-block-size: var(--detail-header-h)` on medium and wider. Its content sits bottom-aligned in the left 5 of 12 columns, `--space-8` from the floor (§6). Gaps use `--space-*`.
- `seriesLink` comes first. It renders as `<div data-spatial-row>` holding one link with `data-roving-item`, driven by `useRovingFocus("row", { spatial: true })`. The text is `--type-label`, `--text-1`, underlined on hover and focus.
- `<h1 ref tabIndex={-1} className="detail-title">` wraps `LogoTitle`. The Logo is capped by `--hero-logo-max-w` and `--hero-logo-max-h`, reusing the P13-08 tokens. The text title uses `--type-hero` and is clamped to two lines.
- Facts use `--type-meta`, then badges, then actions, then notice, then overview.
- All header text uses `--text-1`, as P13-08 Decision 2 does. The P13-08 contrast proof (`tokens.test.ts`, Approach 8 of that spec) covers `--text-1` inside the 5/12 column under `--scrim-hero`. The ambient layer uses the same `--scrim-hero` and `--scrim-floor` stack (`styles.css:179`).
- Badges sit on opaque `--surface-2`.

### 4. Facts (`detail-facts.ts`)

`detailFacts(item)` joins its parts with " · " and returns `undefined` when there are none:

- Episode: `S{season} · E{episode}` when both numbers are known (§10), then `runtimeLabel(runTimeTicks)`, then the first two genres.
- Movie and others: the year, `runtimeLabel`, then the first two genres. Two genres matches the Home hero (`hero-facts.ts:18`).
- No rating (Q8 A).

### 5. Badges (`media-badges.ts`)

`mediaBadges(version)` returns at most five strings, in this order (§7.9):

1. `"4K"` when `video.width >= 3200`. Move `FOUR_K_WIDTH` and `isFourK` here from `hero-facts.ts`.
2. The range label: Dolby Vision variants become `"Dolby Vision"`, and `HDR10`, `HDR10+`, `HLG`, and `HDR` stay as they are. `SDR` and `Unknown` add no badge. Move `rangeLabel` here.
3. The audio badge for the default audio track, else the first audio track: `codecName` plus `channelLabel`, for example "TrueHD 7.1".
4. `"Atmos"` only when that track's `title` matches `/atmos/i`.
5. One subtitle badge that joins the distinct image or styled subtitle formats among `pgssub`, `dvdsub`, `dvd_subtitle`, `ass`, and `ssa`, mapped by `codecName` (for example `"PGS · ASS"`).

Badges never use third-party logos.

`versionSummary(version)` is the Picker option `description`: the badges joined by " · ", for example "4K · Dolby Vision · TrueHD 7.1". The option `label` stays the server's version name.

Badges follow the chosen version. Fixture check: `m1`'s `src-4k` gives `4K`, `Dolby Vision`, `TrueHD 7.1`, `Atmos`, `PGS · ASS` (`mock-server.ts` `hdrSource`).

### 6. Action bar (`ActionBar` and `detail-actions.tsx`)

`ActionBar` is `<div className="ui-action-bar" role="group" aria-label="Actions" data-spatial-row>` with `useRovingFocus("row", { spatial: true })`. Children opt in with `data-roving-item`. Left and Right move between them. Down goes to the next spatial row (the strip), and Up goes to the series link.

Order (§7.8 as amended by Q6 A and Q7 A):

1. Primary, a `ButtonLink variant="primary"`:
   - When `resumeTicksOf(item)` is set, the label is "Resume from {positionLabel}" and a `ProgressRing` shows the resume fraction `resumeTicks / runTimeTicks`. No ring without a runtime.
   - Otherwise the label is "Play".
   - The link keeps the `PlayLink` click rule (`play-actions.tsx:49`; reuse `playActivation` from `card-view.ts`). Its `href` is `playRoute({ itemId, mediaSourceId: selected.id, startTicks })`.
2. The version `Picker`, only with 2+ versions (Q6 A). It uses `label="Version"`, `hideLabel`, `appearance="pill"`, `rovingItem`, options `{ value: id, label: name, description: versionSummary }`, and `onChange` = `setChosenId`.
3. Start over, a `ButtonLink variant="secondary"`, only when resuming. Same route with `startTicks: 0`.
4. `ToggleButton` "Watched", then `ToggleButton` "Favorite". Each uses `aria-disabled` while pending and ignores clicks then, as `watch-state.tsx:52` does today.

There is no `More` menu (Q7 A).

Under the bar, outside the roving row, two kinds of message can appear:

- The player-unavailable notice. It keeps today's `role="alert"` and its exact copy "The player cannot start, so Play will fail: {safeReason}". "Check again" becomes `Button variant="quiet"` inside its own single-item `data-spatial-row` (`useRovingFocus("row", { spatial: true })`, the button carries `data-roving-item`), so arrow keys reach it from the action bar.
- Flag errors as `role="alert"`. Their copy becomes "Couldn't update watched state. Try again." and "Couldn't update favorite. Try again." (§10 voice). The unit test assertion text changes to match. They stay inline, not as a `Toast`, because no `ToastProvider` is mounted in the app (only `gallery-view.tsx`). See Out of scope.

`ProgressRing` draws a 4 px-wide circle track in `--on-light` at 30% and the fill in `--on-light`. It does not use `--art-accent`, because the accent is light and the primary fill is `--text-1`, so the accent would fail 3:1. The ring is decorative; the label carries the position. Under reduced motion there is no animation.

### 7. `ToggleButton` (shared)

Props: `pressed`, `onPressedChange`, `label`, `iconOff: LucideIcon`, `iconOn: LucideIcon`, `filledWhenOn?: boolean`, plus native button props such as `aria-disabled` and `data-roving-item`.

It renders `<button type="button" aria-pressed className="ui-button ui-button--secondary ui-toggle" data-icon={pressed ? "on" : "off"}>`. Inside are the icon (20 px, `aria-hidden`, and `fill="currentColor"` when on and `filledWhenOn`) and the visible label.

State shows by icon and ARIA (§11.4). The pressed fill may step up one surface level, but colour is never the only cue. It is 44 px or taller (`--control-h` is 48 px).

### 8. `FileAndStreams` (`file-and-streams.tsx`)

`<section aria-labelledby>` with an `h2` "File and streams":

- With 2+ versions, a line "Version: {name}" names the version the picker chose.
- Then `MediaInfo`. It keeps its `<section aria-label="Media info for {name}">` and the same `dt`/`dd` content, so e2e assertions stay valid.
- The `dl` sits in two columns (`grid-template-columns: max-content 1fr`, wrapping to one column on compact windows). Values use `font: var(--type-mono)`.

With no versions, it shows "No media information for this item." as today.

### 9. Episode strip (`episode-strip.tsx`)

It renders only when `item.type === "Episode"` and both `seriesId` and `seasonId` are present.

- The query is `useQuery({ queryKey: seasonEpisodesKey(...), queryFn: gateway.episodes(seriesId, seasonId, signal), gcTime: 0 })`.
- The items are `toMediaRowItems(cards.filter((c) => c.id !== item.id), "thumb", imageUrlFor(gateway))`. Each card links to `itemRoute` through `cardRoute` (Q9 A).
- The title is `More from ${seasonName}`. Without a name it is `More from Season ${seasonNumber}`. Without a number it is "More from this season".
- `MediaRow` handles pending (skeleton), error (`InlineError` "Couldn't load More from Season 1." with Retry), and hidden when empty.

The strip keeps series order. Positioning the row at the current episode is out of scope.

### 10. `Notice` (shared)

Props: `title`, `context?` (the item title, `--type-meta`), `children` (the explanation, `--type-body-lg`), `whatYouCanDo?: ReactNode`, `actions: ReactNode`, `headingRef`, and `headingLevel?: 1 | 2` (default 1).

Layout follows §7.16: left-aligned within five columns and vertically centred in the viewport below the nav. It takes no art of its own. A caller that wants the dimmed item backdrop wraps it in `AmbientBackdrop`, which is what P13-15 will do.

Detail uses it without art, since a missing item has no artwork. The title is "This item is no longer on the server", the body is "It may have been removed or renamed.", and the action is `ButtonLink variant="primary" href="#/next/home"` "Go home".

### 11. `PersonRow` (shared)

`<section aria-labelledby>` with an `h2` "Cast", then `<ul className="ui-person-row">`, a wrapping grid of cards (`repeat(auto-fill, minmax(var(--poster-w), 1fr))`). It is not a horizontal scroller, because a scroller of non-focusable cards cannot be scrolled from the keyboard (§11.7).

Each card has:

- A 64 px initials disc (`--surface-2`, `--type-label`, `aria-hidden`).
- The name (`--type-label`, `--text-1`).
- The role (`--type-meta`, `--text-2`), as plain text with no "as".

The cards are not interactive (Q8 A).

`initials(name)` takes the first letters of the first and last words, upper-cased, and handles a single word and extra whitespace.

### 12. Overview (`overview.tsx`)

A `p` clamped to three lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 3`; `--type-body-lg`; `max-inline-size: var(--measure)`). A `useLayoutEffect` compares `scrollHeight` with `clientHeight`, and also reruns on resize. When the text overflows, a quiet `Button` "More" with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` follows. Activating it removes the clamp, and the label becomes "Less".

The button is a single-item spatial row, so arrows reach it. Without overflow there is no button.

### 13. Home prefetch (§9; P13-08 follow-up, owner P13-09)

In `home-view.tsx`, the dwell callback that already sets the ambient art (`home-view.tsx` `onItemFocus`) also calls `queryClient.prefetchQuery({ queryKey: detailKey(gateway, card.id), queryFn: ({ signal }) => gateway.itemDetail(card.id, signal), staleTime: 30_000, gcTime: 30_000 })` for cards whose `kind` is `Movie` or `Episode`. Series cards lead to the series page, so they are not prefetched.

The detail screen keeps `gcTime: 0`. TanStack Query keeps the larger gcTime of a query's observers (`@tanstack/query-core` 5.101.4 `removable.js:18` `updateGcTime` uses `Math.max`), so a prefetched entry lasts 30 s. Mounting the detail screen then shows it at once and refetches it, because the default `staleTime` is 0.

Play stays one activation, and the backdrop half already happens through the ambient layer.

5. The "Back to home" link and the "Details" heading are removed (§12). The nav and browser Back replace them.

7. Cast wraps instead of scrolling sideways, because its cards are not focusable (§11.7).
