## Goal

Auto-play next is fixed today: the Up Next card always counts down from `AUTO_PLAY_SECONDS = 10` (`src/features/player/auto-play-next.ts:3`) and advances at zero or at the end of the file (`src/features/player/index.tsx:170-174`, `:186-193`). PRODUCT.md promises the viewer can turn it off and set its length ("In a series the next episode plays after a countdown you can see, hide, or turn off", `PRODUCT.md:37`; "auto-play next on or off and its countdown length", `PRODUCT.md:107`). DESIGN.md §7.18 places both rows in Settings › Playback and says the switch control arrives with this task (`DESIGN.md:343`, `:350`).

This task adds two preferences for this computer, "Play next episode automatically" (on by default) and "Countdown" (5, 10, or 15 s; default 10), stores them in the P3-22 preferences store, and makes the player obey them: with auto-play off the card still appears at the credits with Play now and Hide but nothing counts or advances; with auto-play on, the chosen length drives the card's start time, the countdown, the fill, and the announcements.

## Risks and out of scope

Risks:

- **Design calls not yet made (Q1 to Q4).** The switch has no visual spec, and the card without a countdown, the EOF destination, and the copy are not specified. M2 and M3 wait for the answers; guessing would conflict with the owner's rule that the user makes design calls.

Keeping the last frame and the card on screen after EOF needs the native engine to hold at the end (mpv `keep-open`) and is out of scope (Q2 option C).
- **Setting read once per session.** A change in Settings applies from the next Play. Settings is not reachable from the player, so this is not visible; noted in the decision log.
- **Clip shorter than the countdown.** `cardStartSeconds` can be negative for very short files (already true for 10 s; the e2e clip is 25 s). Unchanged behaviour; the 15 s choice only widens it.
- **Timing e2e runtime.** The off journey waits 17 s and the 15 s journey about 15 s of real time; budget is within the existing per-test timeouts.
- **`docs/security.md`** wording changes; it is factual and the store's allowlist rule is unchanged.

Out of scope:

- Skip settings (P3-24), volume (P3-26), any other preference.
- Preparing the next episode during the countdown (backlog D7, proposed P3-29).
- Auto-play for movies or across series ("Nothing unrelated starts by itself").
