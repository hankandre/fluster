## Goal

Make the series page read like the show. Today the page `h1` is the route name "Series" (`src/features/browse/series-view.tsx:14`). There is no art, overview, or play action. Seasons are plain buttons whose selection shows only through `aria-pressed` and no visible mark (`series-view.tsx:43`). Only the 16:9 art is a link; it opens the episode's detail, and the title and overview beside it are plain text (`series-view.tsx:78`). A developer link "Back to libraries" goes to the browse index. See audit.md "Series, seasons, and episodes" (three P0s, one P1, one P2) and Top 3.

After this task:

- The series name or Logo is the `h1` over the full-bleed backdrop. The header reuses the P13-09 `DetailHeader`: a facts line ("2022–2024 · 2 seasons · Drama · Mystery"), an `ActionBar` with one primary action, and the three-line overview (DESIGN.md §7.7, series variant).
- The primary action plays the series' next episode in one activation. It reads "Resume S1 · E2" with a progress ring when that episode is part-watched, "Play S1 · E3" when it is not, and "Play S1 · E1" when the server has no next episode (DESIGN.md §10; decisions-P13 "Not asked": "a fully watched series offers Play S1 · E1").
- Seasons are `SegmentedTabs` with a visible selection bar and `aria-selected`, Specials last. The selected tab on open is the next episode's season, else the first regular season (Q10 A).
- Episodes are `EpisodeRow`s (DESIGN.md §7.6). Each row shows the thumb with progress and a played check, "2 · The Keeper's Log", the runtime or time left, and a two-line overview. Text and art never overlap. Enter or click anywhere on the row plays the episode. A trailing Details icon link and the `i` key open the episode's detail.
- Follow-ups that name this task land here: the card subtitle "S1E2 · Pilot" becomes "S1 · E2 · Pilot" (rework-plan.md line 166), and the episode click target covers the title and overview (line 147).

## Approach

### 1. Page frame and states (`series-view.tsx`)

`SeriesView({ seriesId })`:

- Reads `useSession()` and redirects to `#/next/sign-in` without a gateway, as `ItemDetailView` does.
- Renders `<main className="detail-page">`, which gives the same stacking context and `--ambient-h: var(--detail-header-h)`.
- Inside, renders `<DetailBackdrop gateway>{(onArt) => <SeriesContent key={seriesId} ... onArt />}</DetailBackdrop>`, so moving between series crossfades.

`SeriesContent` queries `detailKey(gateway, seriesId)` with `gateway.itemDetail` and `gcTime: 0`. That is the same key as Detail, so a series opened from a Detail series link or a Home prefetch can reuse a warm entry. It switches on the state:

- Pending: `DetailSkeleton` (visually hidden `h1` "Loading details", skeletons after 150 ms).
- `ItemNotFoundError`: `ItemGoneNotice` ("This item is no longer on the server" / "It may have been removed or renamed." / "Go home"; §10 "Item gone").
- Other error: `DetailLoadError`.
- Success: `SeriesBody`.

Focus: `useHeadingFocus(!query.isPending)` focuses the `h1` once, when the state first settles (§9, desktop). One Tab then reaches the primary action.

The `h1` "Series" and the "Back to libraries" link go away (DESIGN.md §12 "Route-named pages", "developer links in flows"; audit P2). The nav and browser Back replace them.

### 2. Header (`series-body.tsx`, `series-facts.ts`)

`SeriesBody({ gateway, item, onArt, headingRef })`:

- `useHeaderArt(item, gateway, onArt)` sets the ambient art and returns `inlineArt`. `detailArt` already handles a series: backdrop, else the poster beside the text.
- `DetailHeader` props:
  - `title={item.title}`
  - `logo={item.logo ? { src: imageUrl(item.logo, 800) } : undefined}`. A series' own Logo names the series, unlike an episode's.
  - `facts={seriesFacts(item, seasons.data)}`
  - `actions={<SeriesActions ... />}`
  - `notice={nextUpError}`
  - `overview={item.overview ? <Overview text={item.overview} /> : undefined}` (reused from `../detail/overview`)
  - `inlineArt`
  - `headingRef`
- No `MediaBadges`: a series has no file.
- Below the header, `<div className="detail-body">` holds `SeriesSeasons` on the opaque `--surface-0` sheet (P13-09 Decision 4).

`seriesFacts(item, seasons?)` joins its parts with " · " and returns `undefined` when there are none:

1. The year range. When `endYear` is set and greater than `year`, it is `"{year}–{endYear}"` (en dash). Otherwise it is `"{year}"`.
2. The season count, counting only seasons whose `indexNumber !== 0`: `"1 season"` or `"{n} seasons"`. It is omitted while the seasons are loading or when the count is 0.
3. The first two genres (Q8 A; same cap as `detail-facts.ts`).

No rating (Q8 A).

`SeriesActions` is an `ActionBar` with one child:

- Next Up and the fallback still pending: a `Skeleton` block at `--skeleton-action-w` × `--control-h`. A pending primary has no link, so there is nothing to activate early.
- A primary from `seriesPrimary`: `ButtonLink variant="primary" data-roving-item href={playRoute(route)} onClick={playActivation(route)} icon={progress !== undefined ? <ProgressRing value={progress} /> : undefined}`.
- No primary (Next Up failed, or the series has no episodes): the bar is not rendered.

There is no Start over, Watched, Favorite, version picker, or `More` (Q7 A; acceptance asks for one Play or Resume button).

The Next Up error is `<InlineError message="Couldn't load your next episode." onRetry={() => void nextUp.refetch()} />` in the `notice` slot. `InlineError` gets the opt-in `spatial` prop, which wraps Retry in a single-item `data-spatial-row` with `data-roving-item`, so arrow keys reach it. The seasons and episodes Retries use `spatial` too. `series-view.test.tsx` shows ArrowUp from the tabs reaching Retry when Next Up fails.

### 3. Gateway: `seriesNextUp`

```ts
async seriesNextUp(seriesId: string, signal?: AbortSignal): Promise<SeriesNextUp | undefined>
```

It sends `GET /Shows/NextUp` with these params:

- `userId`
- `seriesId`
- `limit: 1`
- `enableUserData: true`
- `enableResumable: true`
- `enableRewatching: false`
- `enableImages: false`
- `enableTotalRecordCount: false`

It uses `CONTROL_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS`, and returns `toSeriesNextUp(data.Items?.[0])`. Each parameter exists on @jellyfin/sdk 1.0.2 `ShowApiGetNextUpRequest` (`show-api.d.ts:64`).

`enableUserData` is needed for the resume position. Home's `nextUp` does not send it, which rework-plan.md logs as unverified. `enableImages: false` keeps the response small, because the header shows no art for the episode. No `fields`: only `Id`, `Name`, `Type`, `SeasonId`, `ParentIndexNumber`, `IndexNumber`, `RunTimeTicks`, and `UserData` are read, and each is a base field.

`toSeriesNextUp(item)`:

- Returns `undefined` unless `Id`, `Name`, and `Type === "Episode"` are present.
- Maps `seasonId` from `SeasonId`, and `seasonNumber` and `episodeNumber` when both numbers are present.
- Sets `resumePositionTicks` when not `Played` and the position is above 0.
- Sets `progress = min(1, position / RunTimeTicks)` when both are above 0.

Nothing here builds a media URL or touches Playback Negotiation. The route to the player is the existing app route.

### 4. Primary action (`series-next.ts` `seriesPrimary`)

`seriesPrimary(nextUp: SeriesNextUp | undefined, fallback: EpisodeCard | undefined, fallbackSeason?: Season): SeriesPrimary | undefined` returns `{ label, route: PlayRoute, progress? }`:

- With `nextUp` and `resumePositionTicks`:
  - The label is `"Resume S{s} · E{e}"`, or `"Resume"` without both numbers.
  - The route is `{ itemId, startTicks: resumePositionTicks }`.
  - `progress` is `nextUp.progress` when set.
- With `nextUp` and no resume: the label is `"Play S{s} · E{e}"` or `"Play"`, and the route is `{ itemId, startTicks: 0 }`.
- Without `nextUp` (the server says nothing is next, for example a fully watched series):
  - With a `fallback` (the first episode of the season `initialSeason(ordered, undefined)` opens: the first regular season, else `ordered[0]`, so a Specials-only series still gets a Play button), the label is `"Play S{s} · E{e}"` and the route starts at 0. The season number is `fallback.seasonNumber`, else `fallbackSeason.indexNumber`. The episode number is `fallback.episodeNumber`.
  - Without a `fallback`, it returns `undefined`.

The fallback episode list uses `episodesKey` of that season, the same key as the list. It is `enabled` only when Next Up succeeded with `undefined`. When the first regular season is also the selected tab, no second request is made.

No `mediaSourceId` is sent, so the server picks the version, as Home's one-press play does (`cardPlayRoute`).

### 5. Season order and the opening tab (`series-next.ts`)

- `orderSeasons(seasons)`: a stable partition. Seasons with `indexNumber !== 0` (including `undefined`) come first, in server order, and seasons with `indexNumber === 0` come last.
- `initialSeason(ordered, nextUp)` (Q10 A) tries these in order and returns the first match:
  1. The season whose `id === nextUp.seasonId`.
  2. The season whose `indexNumber === nextUp.seasonNumber`. This mirrors `episode-strip.tsx` `findSeasonId` for servers that omit `SeasonId`.
  3. The first season with `indexNumber !== 0`.
  4. `ordered[0]`.
- When Next Up failed, `nextUp` is `undefined`, so the first regular season opens.

### 7. `SegmentedTabs` `spatial` prop

When `spatial` is true:

- The tablist also carries `data-spatial-row` and gets the `ref`, `onFocus`, and `onKeyDown` of `useRovingFocus("row", { spatial: true })`. The hook is always called, and its handlers are attached only when `spatial` is set.
- Only the selected tab carries `data-roving-item`. A spatial move from the action bar or the episode list therefore always lands on the selected tab (APG: focus entering a tablist goes to the active tab). The roving `sync` sets that one tab's `tabindex` to 0, which agrees with the primitive's own `stop`.
- `onKeyDown` runs the primitive's `move` first. For Left, Right, Home, and End, `move` calls `preventDefault`, so the roving handler returns early (`roving-focus.ts:115`). Up and Down reach the roving handler. In the row layout it finds no next index, so it moves to the adjacent spatial row.

Only `activation="automatic"` is supported with `spatial`. In manual mode the focused tab may not be the selected one, so Up and Down do nothing there. Settings (P13-17) can extend this.

### 8. `EpisodeRow` and `EpisodeList` (shared)

`EpisodeList({ label, children })` is `<ul className="episode-list" aria-label={label} data-spatial-row>` with `useRovingFocus("grid", { spatial: true, columns: 2 })`:

- Each row has exactly two roving items in DOM order: the play link, then the Details link.
- Down and Up move to the same column in the next or previous row.
- Left and Right move between a row's play link and its Details link.
- Up from the first row moves spatially to the selected tab.
- The list is one Tab stop (§9).
- `columns: 2` replaces the `offsetTop` measurement, which cannot work when the two items sit at different heights.

`EpisodeRow` props:

- `id`, `title`
- `number?` (the episode number)
- `subtitle?`: kept only for the accessible name ("Episode 1")
- `runTimeTicks?`, `remainingTicks?`, `progress?`, `played?`
- `overview?`
- `image?: { src?: string; blurHash?: string }`
- `href`, `onActivate?`, `detailsHref`

Markup:

```tsx
<li className="ui-episode-row">
  <a className="ui-episode-row-link" href onClick={onActivate} data-roving-item data-media-card="" data-variant="episode"
     aria-label={cardAccessibleName(...)} aria-describedby={`${metaId} ${overviewId}`} aria-keyshortcuts="i"
     onKeyDown={i or ContextMenu → location.hash = detailsHref} onContextMenu={same}>

    <div className="episode-text" aria-hidden="true">

      <p id={metaId} className="ui-episode-row-meta">{timeLeft ?? runtime}</p>
      <p id={overviewId} className="ui-episode-row-overview">{overview}</p>
    </div>
```

- `cardAccessibleName` is the `accessibleName` rule exported from `media-card.tsx`: title, subtitle, time left spoken, "watched", joined by ", ". Today's names ("First Season Opener, Episode 1") therefore stay, and e2e name selectors keep working.
- `aria-describedby` points at the meta line and the overview. Elements hidden with `aria-hidden` can still be referenced for a description (accname 1.2), so a screen reader hears the runtime and overview after the name. A meta line or overview that is absent is left out of the id list.
- Layout:
  - The `li` is a grid: `minmax(0, 1fr) auto` (link, Details) with `align-items: start`.
  - The link is a grid: `var(--thumb-w) minmax(0, 1fr)`, with `gap: var(--space-4)`.
  - At `max-width: 719px` the link becomes one column: the frame, then the text. The frame keeps `var(--thumb-w)`, so the `cards.spec.ts` frame-width check holds.
- Text tokens:
  - Title: `--type-label`, `--text-1`, one line, ellipsis.
  - Meta: `--type-meta`, `--text-2`.
  - Overview: `--type-body`, `--text-2`, `-webkit-line-clamp: 2`, `max-inline-size: var(--measure)`.
- Hover tints the title `--text-1` and half-lifts the frame, as §7.5 does for cards. Focus draws the ring on the frame (focus layer rule in `media.css`).
- Do not put the `.media-card` class on the link: `.media-card[data-variant="episode"]` fixes the width to `--thumb-w` (`media.css:15`).
- No colour, size, or duration is hard-coded.

## Decisions

Settled by the human in `docs/specs/decisions-P13.md`:

- Q10 A: the opening tab is the next or resumable episode's season, else the first regular season. The tabs wait for the Next Up result.
- Q8 A: no rating in the facts line; genres join it.

Resolved by this spec from DESIGN.md, the code, and the plan (not new design calls):

1. The next episode comes from `/Shows/NextUp?seriesId=` (Q10 A names "the Next Up result"). Its fallback is the first regular season's first episode, the same rule Home's hero uses.
2. The labels are "Resume S1 · E2" and "Play S1 · E3", exactly as §7.7 and §10 write them, with the P13-09 progress ring on Resume. The resume position is not in the label; the ring shows progress.
3. The `EpisodeRow` accessible name keeps the card rule ("Landfall, Episode 1, watched"). The runtime and overview become its description. This keeps names stable for assistive technology and e2e, and the visible text follows §7.6.
4. The whole row (art, title, meta, overview) is the play link, which fixes the click-target follow-up. Details is a separate icon link, because interactive content cannot nest in a link.
5. The episode list is one Tab stop, a two-column roving grid (play, Details) (§9 "Containers are one Tab stop").
6. Only the selected tab is a spatial target, so Up and Down into the tabs land on the active tab (WAI-ARIA APG tabs).
7. The year range shows "2022–2024" only when an end year is known and differs. Otherwise it shows the start year. No "present" is invented.
8. The season count leaves out Specials.
9. When Next Up fails, the header shows `InlineError` with Retry and no primary, rather than guessing S1 · E1 for a viewer who may be mid-series.
10. The "Back to libraries" link and the "Series" heading are removed (§12, audit P2), as P13-09 did on Detail.
11. The card subtitle change to "S1 · E2" lands here, because rework-plan.md names P13-10 its owner.
